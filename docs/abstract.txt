EduPredict: Data-Driven Prediction of Student Academic Performance

Abstract:
In the evolving landscape of higher education, the ability to predict student success and intervene early is a significant challenge for educational institutions. This project, EduPredict, develops a machine learning framework to forecast academic performance and identify at-risk students using behavioral and academic data. Utilizing a synthetic dataset of 500 SRM Institute of Science and Technology students, the study incorporates various predictors such as attendance percentage, internal assessment scores, assignment completion rates, and extracurricular engagement. 

The methodology involves comprehensive data preprocessing, feature engineering (including a weighted performance index), and the application of supervised learning models. Results demonstrate that ensemble methods like Random Forest provide superior predictive accuracy (~92%) compared to traditional logistic regression. The feature importance analysis highlights that early internal assessment scores and consistent attendance are the primary drivers of final academic grades. This research provides a scalable solution for educational analytics, enabling proactive student support systems that align with SDG Goal 4 (Quality Education). By automating the identification of underperforming students, institutions can implement targeted mentorship programs, ultimately improving retention rates and academic outcomes for the Data Science AIML-B 2026 cohort and beyond.
